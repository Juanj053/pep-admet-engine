Pharmacokinetics & Systemic Half-Life
=====================================

Predicts in-vivo biological half-life (t1/2) and systemic clearance dynamics in mammalian
circulation. Models molecular weight cutoffs against the glomerular filtration barrier
(<5 kDa vs >30 kDa), factors in proteolytic degradation penalties, and estimates
serum albumin (HSA) reversible binding propensities to project steady-state volume of
distribution (Vss) and total clearance (CL).
