Membrane Permeability & Bioavailability
=======================================

Assesses cellular uptake, transcellular passive permeability, and oral bioavailability.
Calculates topological polar surface area (TPSA), logP / hydropathicity (GRAVY),
hydrogen bond donors (HBD) and acceptors (HBA), rotatable bonds, and net charge at pH 7.4.
Projects apparent Caco-2 permeability (log Papp), Blood-Brain Barrier penetration (log BB),
and cell-penetrating peptide (CPP) propensity.
