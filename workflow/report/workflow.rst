PEP-ADMET Engine: In-Silico ADMET & Stabilization Workflow
==========================================================

Automated, end-to-end computational pipeline for the preclinical profiling and
rational stabilization of therapeutic peptides and peptidomimetics. The workflow
screens peptide candidates against digestive, serum, and lysosomal proteases,
evaluates systemic elimination half-life and renal clearance thresholds, computes
membrane permeability and oral bioavailability descriptors, formulates actionable
medicinal chemistry stabilization strategies, and synthesizes multi-panel
visual analytics and tabular reports.
