Rational Peptidomimetic Stabilization Advisor
=============================================

Formulates actionable chemical modifications to transform labile peptides into robust
drug leads. Prioritizes N-terminal capping (acetylation, Aib), C-terminal amidation,
stereochemical inversion (D-amino acid substitution at scissile bonds), cyclization
engineering (disulfide, hydrocarbon stapling), and lipid tail conjugation (palmitoylation
via mini-PEG spacers for albumin binding).
