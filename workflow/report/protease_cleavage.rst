Proteolytic Cleavage Profiling
==============================

Quantifies susceptibility to enzymatic degradation across serum, gastrointestinal,
and lysosomal proteases (Trypsin, Chymotrypsin, Pepsin, Elastase, Thermolysin,
Proteinase K, DPP-IV, Thrombin, Factor Xa, Neprilysin, Cathepsins). Outputs detected
scissile bonds, cleavage windows (Schechter & Berger P4-P4' nomenclature),
proteolytic fragments, molecular masses, and a normalized Proteolytic Vulnerability
Index (PVI).
